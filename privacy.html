Privacy Policy

Last updated October 29, 2026

Orbit Health Baby is built to keep your information on your phone. Pregnancy information is personal, so this policy explains plainly what the app stores, what it never collects, and how to contact us.

The short version

  • Everything you enter stays on your iPhone. Three Pound LLC does not receive it and has no servers that store it.
  • There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
  • We do not sell or share your information, because we never have it.
  • Deleting the app deletes everything it stored.

What the app stores on your phone

To work, Orbit Health Baby saves the following on your device only:

  • Your profile: your name (optional), age, height, your weight before pregnancy, your due date, and the daily targets worked out from them or set by you.
  • Your weigh-ins over time.
  • Your food log, favorites, recents, and any custom foods you create or scan from a nutrition label.
  • Your prenatal vitamin and other supplements: the label amounts you enter and which days you took them.
  • How you felt each day, and the notes and symptoms you write for each day.
  • Your app preferences, such as appearance and meal names.

Apple Health (optional)

If you choose to connect Apple Health, Orbit Health Baby reads your step count and your Activity ring data (Move, Exercise and Stand) so it can show them on your Home screen. This is optional, and iOS asks for your permission first, showing exactly what will be read. You can turn it off in Settings, or in the Health app under Sharing.

Orbit Health Baby only reads this data. It never writes to Apple Health. It shows the numbers on your screen and does not save, copy, upload, share, or sell them, and it does not use Health data for advertising or marketing.

Camera and photos (scanning a nutrition label)

If you scan a nutrition label, the app asks for camera access. You can also choose a photo from your library instead, which does not need any permission.

The picture is read on your phone using Apple's text recognition. It is not saved by the app, not uploaded, and not shared. Only the numbers found on the label are filled into a food for you to check and edit.

Microphone and speech recognition

If you use voice search in Nutrition, the app asks for microphone and speech recognition access. You can say no and still use the whole app.

Speech is turned into text by Apple's Speech framework. Where your device supports it, this happens on the device. Otherwise Apple may process the audio on its servers to transcribe it, under Apple's own privacy policy. Orbit Health Baby only receives the resulting text, does not record or keep the audio, and never receives it.

You can change these permissions any time in the iPhone Settings app.

What we do not collect

Orbit Health Baby does not collect, transmit, or share:

  • Your pregnancy, health, body, food, supplement or note information, including anything from Apple Health.
  • Your location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifier.
  • Analytics, crash reports, or usage data.

Backups and your phone

Your iPhone or iCloud backup, if you have one turned on, may include Orbit Health Baby's data. That backup belongs to you and is controlled by Apple's settings and privacy policy, not by us.

Because the app has no sign-in, anyone who can unlock your phone can open it. If that matters to you, keep your phone locked with a passcode.

If you contact us

If you email ThreePoundLLC@gmail.com, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply to you and to improve the app, and we do not share it. Please do not email us health details you would rather keep private.

Children

Orbit Health Baby is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect anything from anyone, including children. The app asks for your age only to pick the right nutrient targets.

Your rights

Because we do not hold any personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export, or delete on your behalf. You are in full control: you can edit or delete anything inside the app, or delete the app to remove all of its data. If privacy law where you live gives you other rights, contact us and we will help.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and its date before the change takes effect. The current version is always available from Settings in the app.
