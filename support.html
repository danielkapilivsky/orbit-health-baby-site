Support

Help with Orbit Health Baby.

How do I contact you?

Email ThreePoundLLC@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version. We aim to reply within a few business days.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. Orbit Health Baby has no accounts and no servers, so we cannot see or recover your data. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app from your iPhone: that removes everything it stored. You can also edit or delete individual entries, notes and weigh-ins inside the app.

I got a new phone. Can I bring my data?

If you restore your new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup of your old one, Orbit Health Baby's data comes with it. Direct export and import are not available yet.

How do I scan a nutrition label?

On the Nutrition tab, tap the scan button next to the plus, then take a photo of the label or choose one from your library. The numbers it finds are filled in for you to check, name and save. The picture is read on your phone and is not kept.

How do I show my steps and Activity rings?

Turn on Show activity on Home in Settings, or tap Connect Apple Health in the Activity section on Home, then allow Steps and Activity when iOS asks. To stop, turn it off in Settings or manage access in the Health app under Sharing, then Apps, then Orbit Health Baby. Orbit Health Baby only reads this data and never changes it.

Why does voice search ask for permission?

Voice search needs the microphone and speech recognition to turn what you say into a search. You can decline and still use everything else, or change your choice in the iPhone Settings app.

Are the daily targets medical advice?

No. They are general pregnancy guidelines. See the Health Disclaimer and ask your doctor or midwife for advice about you.
