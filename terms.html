Estimates and food data

Daily targets are general guidelines for pregnancy based on your age and how far along you are. They are not personalised medical recommendations, and your doctor or midwife may recommend different amounts for you. The baby size comparisons are traditional approximations, not measurements.

Most built-in food data comes from the U.S. Department of Agriculture, FoodData Central (SR Legacy), and the rest was compiled by us. Values are averages for generic foods and can differ from a specific product. Numbers read from a scanned label can be misread, so always check them against the package.
